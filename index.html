THE STORY

About this Benefit Concert

As Smrithi prepares to begin her junior year of high school, she continues to pursue her two greatest passions: music and science. A defining moment in her journey came during middle school when she attended an event hosted by the American Association of University Women. The experience inspired her interest in STEM and opened her eyes to the importance of supporting and encouraging young women in these fields.

Since then, Smrithi has aspired to give back to the organization that helped shape her goals. This benefit concert brings together her love of music and her commitment to STEM education, providing an opportunity to support AAUW's mission of empowering women and girls through education, advocacy, and opportunity.

Please join us for an evening celebrating Smrithi's musical journey and the community that has helped inspire it. Your attendance and any contribution you choose to make will help support this meaningful cause and further AAUW's important work. Thank you for being part of this special event.

AAUW · TECH TREK aauw-ca.org/tech-trek

One week of STEM that can change a girl's future

The American Association of University Women (AAUW) works to advance equity for women and girls. Its signature program, Tech Trek, is a week-long summer camp that immerses girls entering 8th grade in science, technology, engineering, and math — with hands-on workshops, college-campus life, and inspiring women role models from STEM fields.

Started in California in 1998, Tech Trek delivers the life-changing message that STEM is for girls. Camp fees are sponsored by local AAUW branches through donations, so families pay little or nothing. Your gift tonight helps send more local girls to camp.
